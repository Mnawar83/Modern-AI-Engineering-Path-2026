# About the Creator

## Mnawar Mohammed

**Generative AI Specialist · Entrepreneur · Writer**

Mnawar Mohammed is the creator of **Modern AI Engineering Path 2026**, an independent educational initiative designed to help learners move beyond AI experimentation and understand how production AI systems are engineered.

His work sits at the intersection of generative AI, communication, digital products and entrepreneurship.

## Why I created this path

The AI learning landscape is crowded with disconnected tutorials. Many teach individual tools but leave learners without a mental model for how production systems fit together.

I created Modern AI Engineering Path 2026 around a different question:

> What does someone actually need to understand to build, evaluate, secure and ship useful AI systems today?

That is why the curriculum connects model APIs, structured output, retrieval, evals, agents, MCP, security, observability and deployment into one coherent path.

## Project philosophy

The project is built around a few beliefs:

- AI engineering is software engineering with probabilistic components
- evaluation matters more than impressive demos
- workflows should be preferred to agents when the process is known
- retrieval should be measured independently from generation
- security boundaries belong in code, not prompts
- production systems should be observable, bounded and economically understandable
- learners should build things, not merely watch tutorials
